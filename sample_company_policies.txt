# Acme Global Corporation - Internal Guidelines 2026

## 1. Project Apollo Overview
Project Apollo is an autonomous robotics initiative launched on March 15, 2026.
The lead engineer for Apollo is Dr. Sarah Vance, based in the Zurich Research Lab.
The primary objective of Project Apollo is to reduce warehouse sorting latency by 45% using lightweight edge neural networks.

## 2. Remote Work & Travel Policy
- Team members are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- All international travel requires Level 4 VP approval and must be booked through the Portal at least 14 days in advance.
- The maximum daily meal allowance for business travel is $75 USD ($95 EUR in Europe).

## 3. Security & Data Protection
- Two-Factor Authentication (FIDO2 hardware key) is mandatory for all developer machines.
- Passwords must be at least 18 characters long and rotated every 90 days.
- Production database access requires an approved JIRA security ticket and ephemeral bastion credentials expiring in 2 hours.
